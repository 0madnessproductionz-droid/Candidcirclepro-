Promize's Amazing Animal Adventure

Kids & FamilyChildren's BooksColoring BooksAnimals60 pages

By Michael Patterson · Published by Candid Circle Pro

This is not a coloring book you print out. Promize's Amazing Animal Adventure is an interactive children's book where young readers color ON THE SCREEN, right inside the adventure — that hands-on mechanic is the heart of the book.

Starring Promize Moore, the real granddaughter who inspired it, the story pairs an animal-learning journey with pages children bring to life themselves, color by color. Created by Michael Patterson and published by Candid Circle Pro LLC.
