About Michael Patterson & Candid Circle Pro LLC

Michael Patterson is the author of sixteen published books and the founder of Candid Circle Pro LLC, the Orlando company behind his catalog. Rooted in the west side of Orlando, Florida, he writes across horror, crime fiction, inspirational literature, and children's books — from the seven-book Rio Grande Saga and the supernatural dread of The Forgotten and The Haunting of Lake Eola, to the inspirational I Choose Me! and Promize's Amazing Animal Adventure, the interactive coloring book inspired by his granddaughter. His fiction returns again and again to one conviction: loyalty is never a word — it is a practice. He lives in Orlando with his wife, Stephanie, and their four children.

The Company

Candid Circle Pro LLC is an Orlando, Florida company with two crafts under one roof: a publishing house carrying Michael Patterson's sixteen-book catalog, and Executive Solutions, a property-services team handling turnovers, cleanouts, soft wash, grounds, and janitorial work across the Orlando metro. The brand lockup says it plainly: Michael Patterson @ Candid Circle Pro.
